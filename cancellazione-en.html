Kilòmetro does not keep your data on our servers: they are on your phone and, if you chose to sign in with Google, in your Google Drive. That is why you can delete them yourself, immediately and in full, without asking anyone. If you prefer, write to us and we will help you.

1. Data on the phone

  1. Open Settings → Apps → Kilòmetro → Storage and tap Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  2. The following are deleted: vehicles, expenses, reminders, suppliers, preferences and the diagnostic log.

2. Data in your Google Drive

  1. Open Google Drive with the same account used in the app and delete the Kilòmetro folder (it contains registro, foto, backup and export).
  2. On iPhone, if the logbook is on iCloud: Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Kilòmetro → Delete Data; or delete the files from the Kilòmetro folder in iCloud Drive.
  3. On Android the automatic backup to Drive stops when you disconnect the account from the app's More tab; the copies already saved are deleted together with the folder.
  4. Empty the Drive Bin to remove it permanently.
  5. If you have shared the folder with other people, revoke the sharing from Drive first.

3. The app's access to your Google account

  1. In the app: tab Sync → Disconnect account.
  2. In your Google account: Security → Third-party apps with account access → Kilòmetro → Remove access.

What is deleted and what remains

  • Deleted with the steps above: all the app's data (expense logbook, backups, exported sheets) and the link with your Google account.
  • Nothing remains with us, because we neither receive nor keep copies of your data.
  • If you sent us the diagnostic log by e-mail for support, we delete it within 30 days of your request.

Write to puffettodigitale@gmail.com quoting "Kilòmetro – eliminazione dati" (data deletion): we reply within 30 days. The privacy policy explains in detail which data the app processes.
